Something wrong? These steps fix most problems.

  1. Refresh the page.
  2. Update your browser. Current Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox are supported.
  3. In a private tab nothing is saved; switch to a normal tab.
  4. If the game is slow, close other tabs.
