FAQ

Questions

What kind of game is SimEnergy?

A strategy and management game. You build plants, generate power, watch the price and decide when to sell.

Which devices does it run on?

The browser version opens in a current browser on phones, tablets and computers. The status of the Android and iPhone versions is kept up to date in the Where to play section above.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your progress is stored in this device's browser. Clearing browser data or playing in a private tab removes it.

Are there in-game purchases?

For the store versions, the price and any in-game purchases are listed on the store page. This site does not take payments.

Can I play offline?

The first launch needs a connection. Offline play will be announced here once it is tested.

Who do I contact if something breaks?

Write to hello@aiworkschool.com. Tell us your device and what happened.
