Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-10

In short

This site and the browser version of SimEnergy never ask for your name, email or an account. We use no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and no ad trackers.

Game saves

Game progress is stored in this device's browser storage and is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing browser data.

Visit statistics

We do not collect visit statistics.

Hosting

The site runs on a hosting service, which may keep technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and request time) for security and operations and may set a strictly necessary technical cookie for bot protection.

App versions

The iPhone and iPad app also asks for no account, name or email, and has no ads or trackers. Your save stays on your device and is never sent to us. If you allow notifications (for example, reservoir full or Market of the Day ready), they are scheduled only on your device. If in-app purchases are offered, Apple processes the payment; we never see card details. The privacy details on the store page match this description.
