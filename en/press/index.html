Short description

In SimEnergy you build an energy company. Run solar in Kenya, hydro in Norway, biogas and wind in Germany, and sell power at the hour the price is best.

Long description

SimEnergy is a management game built on the power price that shifts through the day. At noon the sun is plentiful but the price is low; in the evening the price climbs and the sun is gone. Each site gives the player a different tool to catch that gap: batteries in Kenya, the dam gate in Norway, feedstock in German biogas, and the call to stop wind turbines. Tasks, upgrades and loans grow the company.
